# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: March 28, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Video to Photo** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Video files** you import into the App for frame extraction
- **User preferences** such as language, theme, default export quality, and image format

### Automatically Collected Information
- **App usage data** related to advertisements (ad impressions and interactions), collected by third-party ad services
- **Device information** such as device model, operating system version, and screen size, collected by third-party ad services

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal identification information
- We do not collect location data
- We do not use analytics or crash reporting services
- We do not track your app usage behavior

## 2. How Your Data Is Processed

All video processing and frame extraction is performed **locally on your device**. Your videos and extracted photos are never uploaded to our servers or any external servers. Specifically:

- Video frame extraction uses FFmpeg, which runs entirely on your device
- Scene analysis (brightness, sharpness, scene change detection) is performed locally
- Extracted frames are saved to your device's local storage
- Project history and preferences are stored locally using an on-device database (Hive)

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

### Google AdMob
We display banner advertisements and rewarded interstitial advertisements through Google AdMob. Google may collect:
- Device advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android)
- Device characteristics (OS, model, screen size)
- Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
- Approximate location derived from IP address

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit: [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
The App uses Google's UMP SDK to manage your ad consent preferences in accordance with applicable regulations.

## 4. Device Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| Photo Library (Read) | To import videos from your photo library |
| Photo Library (Write) | To save extracted frames to your photo library |
| Storage (Android) | To access video files on your device |

We only request permissions that are necessary for the App's core functionality. You can revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

- All data is stored **locally on your device**
- We do not maintain any servers or databases that store your data
- You can delete your project history and saved image records within the App
- Uninstalling the App will remove all locally stored data

## 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
