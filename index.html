2. How Your Data Is Processed

All video processing and frame extraction is performed locally on your device. Your videos and extracted photos are never uploaded to our servers or any external servers. Specifically:

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I extract frames from a video?
Open the app, tap Import Video, select a video from your gallery or files, use the Frame Explorer to scrub through frames, and tap Save This Frame to capture.
What video formats are supported?
Video to Photo supports all common video formats including MP4, MOV, AVI, and MKV.
How do I change the export quality?
Go to Settings > Default Save Quality and choose between Original, 1080p, or 720p. You can also switch between JPEG and PNG formats.
Can I extract multiple frames at once?
Yes! Select as many frames as you want in the Frame Explorer, then save them all at once.
Where are my extracted photos saved?
All extracted photos are saved directly to your device's photo gallery. You can also view them in the app's built-in Gallery.
How do I delete extracted frames?
Open Gallery, long-press to enter selection mode, select the frames you want to remove, and tap Delete.
